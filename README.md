# The Best Advice I Ever Got

<!-- SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT -->

An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-the-best-advice-i-ever-got-358cefb0) bounty "The Best Advice I Ever Got" — one sentence, at most 200 characters, about the best advice the author ever received.


## The entry

> My grandfather told me never to lend money to family, then lent me his truck, his tools, and twenty bucks — and charged me interest in chores.

The advice is real and specific — "never lend money to family" — and the
sentence earns its keep by showing the adviser breaking his own rule in
the same breath. The truck, the tools and the twenty bucks are the loan;
"interest in chores" is the punchline, and also the truest part: the debt
was paid in Saturday mornings, which is exactly how a grandfather
collects.


## How it runs

`main.py` is a small Python program. It validates the entry — exactly one
sentence, no more than 200 characters — prints it, and writes it to
`/output/entry.txt` for the make.fun harness to grade.

```sh
python3 main.py
```

No dependencies, no network, no randomness: the same input always
produces the same single line.
